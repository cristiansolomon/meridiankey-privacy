Privacy Policy for MeridianKey

Effective date: October 5, 2026
Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

MeridianKey is an educational and wellness application developed and published by Cristian Solomon, based in Bucharest, Romania.

This Privacy Policy explains how MeridianKey handles information when you use the application.

MeridianKey is designed to operate primarily offline. The application does not require the creation of a user account and does not use an online account system or a developer-operated cloud service.

2. Information collected by the application

MeridianKey does not automatically collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

The application does not intentionally collect:

  • names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or postal addresses;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts, photographs, audio, or video;
  • advertising identifiers or persistent device identifiers;
  • financial or payment information;
  • authentication credentials;
  • information from Health Connect;
  • browsing activity;
  • diagnostic, advertising, or analytics data.

MeridianKey does not contain advertising or behavioural-tracking functionality.

3. Information stored locally on the device

Some information may be created and stored locally on your device as part of the application s functionality.

Point activity log

When you intentionally use the Log function, MeridianKey records:

  • the date and time of the entry; and
  • the code of the selected meridian point.

The log may reflect your activity note in the application if you use the log button for some meridian points. It is stored locally within the application s storage and is not automatically transmitted to the developer or to any developer-operated server.

Application settings and locally selected content

Application preferences and other information necessary for the application to function may also be stored locally on your device.

If a version of MeridianKey allows you to select, import, or open a file, that file is processed locally for the requested function. MeridianKey does not automatically upload its contents to the developer.

4. Exporting and sharing information

MeridianKey may allow you to copy or export the activity log.

Exporting occurs only when you initiate the action. The application may use Android s system file picker or sharing functions, allowing you to choose where the exported file is saved or which application or service receives it.

Once you export or share a file, its handling depends on your chosen storage location, application, or third-party service. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. The developer of MeridianKey does not control exported copies and does not automatically receive them.

You are responsible for protecting and deleting exported files from the locations in which you choose to store them.

5. Device backups

Depending on your Android device, Google account, and backup settings, the Android operating system may include locally stored application data in a device or cloud backup.

Such backups are controlled by the operating-system or backup-service provider and not by MeridianKey or its developer. You can manage backup preferences through your device and Google account settings.

6. Information received when you contact the developer

If you contact the developer by email or through another support channel, the developer may receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • your name or email address;
  • the contents of your message;
  • technical details you choose to provide; and
  • attachments you choose to send.

This information will be used only to respond to your request, provide support, investigate a reported issue, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Please do not send medical records, passwords, financial information, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information when requesting support.

7. Google Play and platform information

MeridianKey may be distributed through Google Play. Google may process information concerning application installation, device compatibility, application performance, reviews, or use of Google Play services in accordance with Google s own privacy policies.

The developer may receive aggregated or limited information through Google Play Console, such as installation statistics, application performance information, ratings, reviews, and sales information where applicable.

The processing performed independently by Google is governed by Google s privacy policies and is not controlled by MeridianKey.

8. Sharing and sale of information

The developer does not sell, rent, or use personal information for advertising.

MeridianKey does not automatically share locally stored activity logs or other locally created information with the developer or with third parties.

Information may be disclosed only where required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or where necessary to protect legal rights and security. This does not include information stored only on your device and inaccessible to the developer.

9. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored application information generally remains on your device until:

  • you delete it using an available application function;
  • you clear MeridianKey s storage through Android settings; or
  • you uninstall the application.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its storage normally removes locally stored MeridianKey information from the device. Copies contained in Android or cloud backups may remain subject to the backup provider s retention practices.

Exported files remain in the location selected by you until you delete them.

Support correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, maintain support records, protect legal rights, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of personal information contained in support correspondence by using the contact details below, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

MeridianKey does not provide or require an application account. Therefore, there is no MeridianKey account or server-hosted account data to delete.

10. Data security

MeridianKey is designed to minimise the collection and transmission of information. Local data is protected by the security mechanisms provided by your Android device.

However, no device, storage system, or electronic method is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, screen lock, Google account, backup settings, and exported files.

11. Children s privacy

MeridianKey is not specifically directed to children under the age of 14.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, data-handling practices, legal requirements, or Google Play requirements change.

The updated policy will be published at the same public web address, and the Last updated date at the beginning of the policy will be revised.

Material changes will be communicated through the application or its Google Play listing where appropriate.
